Cookies and tracking

The current local MMDub interface does not set website cookies and does not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or embedded third-party pages. No cookie-consent banner is needed for the current configuration because it does not use nonessential tracking cookies.

Local browser storage

MMDub uses browser local storage for one functional purpose: temporarily saving an unfinished manual Burmese SRT draft for a video job so it can survive a refresh. The draft remains on this browser and is removed after successful import or when the job is deleted. Clearing the browser’s site data also removes it. The completion-sound toggle applies only to the current page session and is not saved as a browser preference.

RedNote browser cookies

When you provide a RedNote link, MMDub first attempts an anonymous download. Only if you check the separate browser-session permission box may it read Chrome or Edge cookies temporarily and send them to RedNote to authenticate that download. These are not cookies set by MMDub, and MMDub does not save a copy in its data folder.

Third-party services

Online speech, optional Gemini translation, RedNote downloads, and setup or model downloads make requests to their respective providers when you use those features. MMDub does not embed their websites in the interface. Their own cookies, storage, and privacy rules are controlled by those providers.

Changes

If analytics, advertising, or other nonessential browser storage is added, the policy must be reviewed and appropriate consent controls added before those features are enabled. Hosting MMDub on a public domain also requires a fresh review for that deployment’s jurisdiction and configuration.
